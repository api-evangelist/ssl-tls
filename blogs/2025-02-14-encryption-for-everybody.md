2025 marks ten years of Let’s Encrypt. Already this year we’ve taken steps to continue to deliver on our values of user privacy , efficiency , and innovation , all with the intent of continuing to deliver free TLS certificates to as many people as possible; to deliver encryption for everybody. And while we’re excited about the technical progress we’ll make this year, we’re also going to celebrate this tenth anniversary by highlighting the people around the world who make our impact possible.
