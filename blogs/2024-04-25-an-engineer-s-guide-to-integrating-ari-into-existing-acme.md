Following our previous post on the foundational benefits of ACME Renewal Information ( ARI ), this one offers a detailed technical guide for incorporating ARI into existing ACME clients. Since its introduction in March 2023, ARI has significantly enhanced the resiliency and reliability of certificate revocation and renewal for a growing number of Subscribers. To extend these benefits to an even broader audience, incorporating ARI into more ACME clients is essential.
