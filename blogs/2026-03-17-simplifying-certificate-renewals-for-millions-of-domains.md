Nick Silverman is a Senior Infrastructure Engineer on the Edge Infrastructure team at Shopify, where he maintains the systems that provision, renew, and publish SSL certificates for millions of merchants’ custom domains. He is also a contributor to the Ruby acme-client gem. The challenge Shopify’s automated certificate management system relied on a static renewal threshold: 30 days before the end of the 90-day lifetime.
